This is the Folio manual: how to start a book, lay it out, check it and export it for Amazon KDP, Kobo and print.

:::caution[Folio is in early development]
The manual describes how Folio is designed to work. The application is being built from the window outwards, so most of what you read here does not exist yet, and there is nothing to download. The pages change as the app does.
:::

- **Getting started** covers installing Folio and making your first book.
- **Walkthrough** takes you through a book from the first spread to the export.
- **Using Folio** explains the window, the page marks, styles, Jax and the keyboard shortcuts.
