Make the page look the way you imagine.

Select a picture and a Picture tab appears in the ribbon: replace, crop, rotate, flip, border, rounded corners, filters, and how words wrap around it.

1. Insert → Shapes and Bubbles for speech bubbles, notes and lines.
2. Insert → Table for recipe ingredients or lesson worksheets.
3. Pages → Page background for a colour, texture, gradient or picture behind the page.
