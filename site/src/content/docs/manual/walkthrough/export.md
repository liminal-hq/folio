One button makes every file.

1. Press Export book (Ctrl+E).
2. Tick where it's going: Kindle, Kobo, KDP paperback, hardcover, a PDF for your local printer, or a PDF for sharing.
3. Read the “Before you send it” checks. Each one says how to fix it.
4. Press Export. The files land in your Books folder.

_Figure: Export with the KDP cover diagram and checks._

:::note[Tip]
Amazon asks for three files for a paperback and eBook: the inside pages, the cover, and the EPUB. Folio makes all three.
:::
