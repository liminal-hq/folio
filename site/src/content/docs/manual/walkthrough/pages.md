Add, reorder and design whole spreads.

Every spread sits in the shelf along the bottom. Click one to open it, or drag it to a new place — an orange line shows where it will land.

1. Press + Add pages at the end of the shelf for two new pages.
2. Or choose Insert → Spread templates for a ready-made layout sized to your book.
3. Right-click a thumbnail to duplicate or remove it.
