Drag things on the page; Folio helps them line up.

Click a picture or text box to select it, then drag. Thin cyan lines appear when it lines up with a margin, the middle of the page, or another box — let go and it snaps there.

1. Drag from the middle to move.
2. Drag a corner to resize. Hold Shift to keep the shape.
3. Hold Alt while dragging to ignore the guides.
4. Use the arrow keys to nudge; add Shift to move ten times further.

_Figure: A picture-book spread with a picture selected._

:::note[Tip]
Pink dashed line: the bleed. Pictures that touch the edge should reach it. Purple dashed lines: safe margins — keep words inside them.
:::
