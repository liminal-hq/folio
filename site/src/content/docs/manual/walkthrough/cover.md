Back, spine and front — printed as one sheet.

The Cover is always first in the shelf. It shows the back, spine and front together. The spine gets wider as you add pages, and everything on the front slides along with it.

1. Click the title, blurb or art to change them.
2. Use the switcher to see Paperback, Hardcover, Dust jacket, Inside covers or the eBook crop.
3. Choose ISBN barcode… to make a real barcode, or let Amazon add one.

_Figure: A dust jacket: flaps, back, spine and front._

:::caution[Barcode area]
The white box on the back is where the barcode goes. Folio warns you if anything drifts into it.
:::
