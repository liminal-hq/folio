Two ways to work with the same book. Switch at the top right of the toolbar.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | Plain words. Styles by name, Smaller / Bigger instead of point sizes, formatting applies to the whole box. |
| Expert | Every number: positions in inches or millimetres, single-word styling, master pages, layers, PDF/X and colour profiles. |

:::note[Tip]
Easy never throws anything away. Settings made in Expert keep working when you switch back.
:::
