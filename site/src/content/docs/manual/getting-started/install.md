Folio runs on Windows 11 and on Linux — GNOME, KDE Plasma and Cinnamon.

:::caution[No downloads yet]
Folio is in early development and has no releases. These are the steps you will follow once it does.
:::

## Windows 11

1. Download Folio-Setup.exe from the Download section.
2. Double-click it. Windows may ask if you trust Liminal HQ — choose Yes.
3. Folio appears in the Start menu. Pin it to the taskbar if you like.

## Linux

1. Download the Flatpak, AppImage, .deb or .rpm — whichever your system uses.
2. Flatpak: open it with your software centre, or run flatpak install folio.flatpak.
3. Folio follows your desktop's light or dark theme and its window buttons.

:::tip[Good to know]
Folio never needs an account or the internet. Your books stay on your computer unless you export them.
:::
