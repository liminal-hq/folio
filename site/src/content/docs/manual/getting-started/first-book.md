From opening the app to a finished spread in about ten minutes.

1. Open Folio. The welcome screen asks what you're making — pick a kind, like Picture book.
2. Choose a book size. The common Amazon sizes are listed for each kind.
3. Choose Easy or Expert. Easy is a great place to start; you can switch any time.
4. Decide whether Jax should help. “Yes, walk me through it” gives you a step-by-step guide.
5. Press Start. You'll see sample pages — change their words and pictures to make them yours.

_Figure: The welcome screen: what you're making, how you like to work, and whether Jax helps._

:::note[Tip]
Everything saves itself. Folio also keeps a recovery copy inside your book file.
:::
