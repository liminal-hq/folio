An optional helper. He never interrupts.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Tip | A gentle hint about what you're doing. |
| This page | A checklist for the current spread. |
| Guide | A step-by-step walk through your whole book. |
| Ask | “How do I…?” — answered from Folio's help, on your computer. |

Click Jax on the status bar to open or close him, or press Ctrl+J. Turn him off completely in Preferences.
