Your book is a single, open file.

A .folio file is a zip you can always unpack: your words as plain text and Markdown, your original pictures, and the layout as readable JSON. Nothing is locked away.

:::tip[Peace of mind]
Back up your Books folder like any other documents. A .folio from today will open in future versions.
:::
